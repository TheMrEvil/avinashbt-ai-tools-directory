## 🧠 Why These Criteria?  
We picked products that are:  
- Widely used or well-reviewed in their category  
- Solving a clear, tangible problem  
- Offering a UI/UX and feature-set that stands out  
- Either mature (trusted) or innovative (new & promising)  
This gives you a balanced view of both “safe bets” and “up-and-comers”.
